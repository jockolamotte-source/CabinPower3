About your history

Readings are saved locally from the moment this version is installed. Foreground updates are requested every 30 seconds; Android may defer background jobs beyond 15 minutes. Graph time uses phone capture time, avoiding inverter clock inconsistencies. Gaps mean no measurements were received.

🌤 Background monitoring

Collect a reading periodically when the app isn't open.

Android JobScheduler runs jobs on an approximately 15-minute minimum cadence; actual execution may be delayed by battery saver, Doze or connectivity. Android force-stop disables background jobs until the app is opened again.

Account & storage

Disconnect clears saved credentials but keeps historical graphs. Clearing history permanently removes saved readings and alert events from this phone.
